Common questions

FAQ

Can you build on land I already own?
Yes. We build custom homes on our clients' lots and handle permits, planning and construction from start to finish.
I don't have land yet. Where do I start?
Start with us. We partner with Sandra Watkins of RE/MAX Town & Country, who specializes in lots, communities and new construction, to help you find the right place first.
Do you build Frank Betz plans?
Yes, it's a specialty. Several of our homes have been featured by Frank Betz Associates, and we can adjust a plan to fit your lot and the way you live.
Can I make changes to a plan?
Yes. We regularly customize layouts, finishes and outdoor spaces. We'll talk through what's possible at your first appointment.
Where do you build?
Throughout North Metro Atlanta and North Georgia. If you're unsure whether your lot is in our area, give us a call.
Planning guide

Building checklist

Print it, check it off, and bring it to your first meeting. Download the checklist (PDF)

Land
  • Choose location, schools and commute
  • Check utilities: water, sewer, power, internet
  • Review lot slope, soil and drainage
  • Understand HOA or zoning rules
Builder
  • Research reputation and past work
  • Verify license, insurance and warranties
  • Ask for references and reviews
  • Clarify pricing and communication
Plan
  • Pick a floor plan for your lifestyle
  • Think about future needs and resale
  • Plan storage and outdoor spaces
  • Customize details where needed
Budget & financing
  • Get pre-approved for financing
  • Plan site costs: grading, septic, driveway
  • Include upgrades and contingencies
Permits & prep
  • Confirm zoning, permits and HOA approval
  • Soil or environmental tests if needed
  • Know your construction timeline
Build & move-in
  • Stay in touch with your builder
  • Track change orders and milestones
  • Final walk-through and punch list
  • Review warranties before move-in
