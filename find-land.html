Land plus a builder

Know what it costs to build before you buy.

A pretty lot can hide an expensive surprise: a long driveway, a well and septic you didn't plan for, or a slope that needs a basement. That's why we look at the land with you before you sign anything.

Sandra Watkins, Riverstone's New Home Guide with RE/MAX Town & Country, specializes in lots, land and new construction. Before you buy

What we check on every piece of land

These are the things that change what it costs to build. We look at all of them with you before you buy.

  • Zoning and restrictionsZoning, setbacks, HOA covenants and minimum home size.
  • WaterCounty or city water at the road, or a well.
  • Sewer or septicSewer tap, or septic and a soil test.
  • Slope and soilSlab or basement, grading and drainage.
  • Flood zoneAny part of the lot in a flood zone or near a creek buffer.
  • Access and drivewayRoad frontage, driveway length and permits.
  • Power and internetHow far service has to run to the house site.
  • ClearingTrees, brush and what site prep will cost.

Questions

Land buying questions

Where can I find land for sale to build on in Cherokee County?
Use the county searches on this page. They show every lot and land listing on the MLS in Cherokee and the other counties Riverstone builds in, updated daily. When one looks good, call or text Sandra Watkins at 770-324-3680 to go see it.
Can you tell me what it will cost to build on a lot before I buy it?
Yes, and you should ask before you sign. We walk the property, look at water, sewer or septic, slope, clearing and the driveway, then price a home on that lot so you know the total, land plus house.
Do I need a real estate agent to buy land?
You don't have to have one, but it helps. Sandra is a land and new construction specialist with RE/MAX Town & Country, and she works hand in hand with Riverstone, so the land search and the build plan stay on the same page.
What should I check before buying land in Cherokee County?
Zoning, setbacks and any HOA rules, whether water and sewer are at the road or you'll need a well and septic, slope and soil, flood zones, access and how much clearing it needs. Our post on buying land in Cherokee County GA walks through each one.
Do you only build in Cherokee County?
No. We build throughout North Georgia and North Metro Atlanta, including Bartow, Gordon, Whitfield, Cobb and Pickens counties.
