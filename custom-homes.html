Why build custom

Built around your land and how you live.

A spec home makes you live with someone else's choices. A custom home starts with yours: the lot, the layout, how many bedrooms you really need, where the coffee goes in the morning, and how big the porch should be for a Georgia summer.

As a custom home builder in North Georgia, we know the ground you're building on. Red clay, sloped lots, wells and septic, and six different counties' permit offices all have their quirks, and after building here since 1997 we've learned them so you don't have to.

You'll work with the same three people the whole way: Jamey Cagle, who founded Riverstone and still walks lots, John O'Donnell, our project manager with more than 30 years on job sites, and Sandra Watkins, your New Home Guide.

What you can customize

Start from a proven Riverstone plan or bring your own, then shape it around your lot and your life. Earlier changes are always easier and cheaper, so we sort these out before we break ground.

  • The floor planMove walls, add a bedroom, open the kitchen or add a bonus room.
  • Basement or slabFull basement, daylight basement or slab, depending on your lot.
  • Porches and outdoor livingFront porches, screened porches, decks and patios.
  • Kitchens and bathsCabinets, counters, islands, tile and fixtures.
  • Exterior lookBoard and batten, stone, farmhouse or craftsman details and colors.
  • Garage and storageTwo or three bays, a side entry, or a detached shop.

Questions

Custom home questions

What does a custom home builder in North Georgia do?
A custom builder designs and builds a home around your lot, your plan and your budget instead of selling you a finished spec house. At Riverstone that means one team handles the plan, selections, permits, site prep, construction and the final walk-through.
Can I bring my own house plan?
Yes. Bring your own plan, start from one of ours, or bring a sketch and we'll connect you with someone who can turn your ideas into a set of plans. Then we price it, fit it to your land and build it.
Do you build custom homes on land I already own?
Yes. We build on our clients' land across Cherokee, Bartow, Gordon, Whitfield, Cobb and Pickens counties. Send us the address first and we'll look at water, sewer or septic, slope and access before you plan around a number. See building on your land.
How much does it cost to build a custom home in North Georgia?
It depends on the size, the plan, your finishes and especially your land, since clearing, driveway, utilities and foundation type can swing the total. Our standard features and options price list show what's included, and a building consultation gets you real numbers for your lot. For the bigger picture, read what's changed in the cost to build in North Georgia.
Where do you build?
Throughout North Metro Atlanta and North Georgia: Canton, Woodstock, Ball Ground, Waleska, Cartersville, Adairsville, Calhoun, Dalton, Jasper and the communities around them.
