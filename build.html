Know your land

We'll be your boots on the ground.

Whether you already own land or found a piece you like, send it to us before you spend a dime. Sandra and the Riverstone team dig into what it takes to build there, so the price you plan on is the price you pay.

  • Plats and surveysRecorded plats, existing surveys, property lines and easements.
  • WaterIs county or city water at the road, or will you need a well?
  • Sewer or septicIs sewer available, or will it need septic and a soil test?
  • Power, internet and accessHow far service has to run, road frontage and where the driveway goes.
  • Zoning and restrictionsZoning, setbacks, HOA covenants and any minimum home size.
  • Slope, soil and drainageGrading and foundation needs, slab or basement, and any flood zone.
  • Clearing and house placementWhere the house sits and what site prep could cost.
  • Room for upgradesBudget space for the options and finishes you want.

How it works

From your land to your keys, in five steps

You never have to manage subcontractors or chase permits. Here's what building with Riverstone looks like, on your land or ours.

  1. Building consultation

    Sit down with our team to talk through your land, budget, plan ideas and timeline before you commit to anything.

  2. Choose your plan and finishes

    Pick one of our proven plans or bring your own. Sandra, your New Home Guide, walks you through every selection and the little things you'll be glad you thought of.

  3. Permits and site prep

    We handle permitting, HOA approval and getting your land ready to build.

  4. Construction

    Our project manager runs the job site from the foundation to the final finish, and keeps you updated along the way.

  5. Walk-through and closing

    We walk the home with you, finish the punch list and see you through closing.

Questions

Building on your land: common questions

Can you build on land I already own?
Yes. We build custom homes on our clients' land and handle permits, planning and construction from start to finish. Send us your land's details and we'll look at water, sewer, slope and access with you up front.
Can I bring my own plan?
Yes. You can start from one of our proven plans or bring your own, and we'll fit it to your lot. We regularly customize layouts, finishes and outdoor spaces.
Where do you build?
Throughout North Metro Atlanta and North Georgia, including Cherokee, Bartow, Gordon, Whitfield, Cobb and Pickens counties. If you're not sure your lot is in our area, call us at 706-514-6637.
What comes standard?
Every Riverstone home includes LVP flooring in the main living areas, granite or quartz countertops, custom cabinetry, tiled baths and more. See the full Standard Features list.
How do I pick my finishes?
After your plan is set, we walk you through your selections, from exterior colors and stone to cabinets, flooring and fixtures, with clear deadlines so construction stays on schedule.
I don't have land yet. Where do I start?
Start with Sandra Watkins, our New Home Guide with RE/MAX Town & Country. She specializes in lots and new construction, and you can search land for sale right here by county. Call or text her at 770-324-3680.
