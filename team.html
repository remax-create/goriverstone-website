About Riverstone

Meet the team

Riverstone Homes is locally owned and has been building in North Georgia since 1997. These are the people who will know your name, your lot and your plan from the first meeting to move-in day.

President & Founder

Jamey Cagle

Building in North Georgia since 1997

Jamey Cagle got his start in building while he was still in high school in Canton, and he never looked back. In 1997 he founded Riverstone Homes with a simple idea: build every home the way you'd build your own.

Nearly 30 years later, Riverstone is still locally owned, and Jamey is still the guy walking lots, meeting families and checking the details. He's built through every kind of market, including the 2007 housing crash, and that experience taught him how to build homes that last. His team builds everything from new and custom homes to barndominiums, room additions, basement finishes and homes on clients' own land.

Jamey is a local business owner through and through. Along with Riverstone Homes, he owns Tackle Barn Outdoors, the bait and tackle shop in Adairsville, and Riverstone Graphics. When he's not on a job site, there's a good chance he's somewhere with a fishing rod in his hand.

Project Manager

John O'Donnell

30+ years on the job site

John runs every Riverstone job site, from the foundation to the final walk-through. He brings more than 30 years of hands-on construction experience to each home, and he's the one keeping subcontractors on schedule and making sure the work is done right before it gets covered up.

When you build with Riverstone, John is the person you'll see on site, and the one who can tell you exactly where your home stands.

New Home Sales

Sandra Watkins

28 years in new construction

Sandra is Riverstone's real estate partner and guides buyers through choosing land, a plan and finishes, all the way to closing. She's with RE/MAX Town & Country and consistently ranks among the top 25 RE/MAX agents in Georgia.

In 28 years of new construction she's worked with first-time buyers, growing families and investors large and small, and with many builders on new home sales and land. If you don't have a lot yet, start with Sandra. She'll help you find the right place to build.
