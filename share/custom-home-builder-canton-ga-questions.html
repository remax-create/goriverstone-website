10 Questions to Ask a Custom Home Builder in Canton GA Before You Sign

Hiring a custom home builder in Canton GA? Ask these 10 questions first: licensing, warranty, building on your land, allowances, change orders and updates.

Sandra WatkinsNew Home Guide, Riverstone Homes

Picking a custom home builder in Canton GA is the biggest hire most families will ever make. You will talk to this company almost every week for the better part of a year, and you will live with their work for decades. So before you sign anything, ask good questions and pay attention to how they answer. Here are the ten I would ask any builder in Cherokee County, Metro Atlanta or North Georgia, including us.

1. Are you licensed in Georgia, and can I check it?

Georgia requires residential and general contractors to be licensed through the State Licensing Board for Residential and General Contractors. You can look up any builder by name or license number on the Georgia Secretary of State website in about two minutes. A good builder will hand you the number without blinking.

2. What does your written warranty cover?

Georgia law and state board rules require a written warranty on new single family homes, and the board sets the minimum. Ask to see the actual document, not a summary. Find out what is covered, for how long, and who you call when something comes up after closing.

3. Can I build on my own land?

Some builders only build in their own subdivisions. If you already own a lot, or want to buy land first, ask up front. At Riverstone, our answer is always the same: your land or ours. Here is how we build on your lot, from plans and permits to the final walk-through.

4. Who handles permits, HOA approval and site prep?

Permits, septic or sewer, grading and HOA design approval can eat weeks if nobody owns them. Ask exactly who handles each one and whether it is in the price. You should never have to chase a permit or manage subcontractors yourself.

5. Who runs the job site day to day?

Ask who your superintendent is and how often they are on site. Then ask who your main point of contact is for questions, selections and updates. Knowing names on day one beats calling a general office line in month five.

6. Can I use one of your plans, or bring my own?

A proven floor plan saves time and surprises. A custom plan gets you exactly what you want. A good builder can do either and will tell you honestly how each choice affects your budget and timeline. You can see the kinds of homes we build on our homes page.

7. What is included, and what is an allowance?

Two quotes can look the same and be thousands apart once you get to cabinets, flooring, lighting and appliances. Ask for a list of standard features and every allowance in writing, so you can compare builders apples to apples.

8. How do you handle changes after we start?

Almost everyone changes something. Ask how change orders are priced and approved, and make sure every change is in writing before the work happens.

9. How will you keep me updated?

Silence is the number one complaint people have about builders. Ask how often you will get updates and photos, and how fast they return calls and texts. Then pay attention to how quickly they answered you while you were still shopping.

10. Can I see homes you have built and talk to past buyers?

Walk a finished home if you can. Look at the trim, the doors, the porch, the little stuff. Ask for a couple of past buyers to call. Our home tour videos are a good place to start before you visit in person.

Quick answers

Do builders in Georgia need a license?

Yes. Residential and general contractors in Georgia are licensed by the state licensing board, and you can verify a license on the Georgia Secretary of State website.

Is a warranty required on a new home in Georgia?

Yes. Georgia law and board rules require a written warranty on new single family homes. Ask your builder for a copy before you sign.

Can I hire a builder if I already own land in Cherokee County?

Many builders, including Riverstone, will build on your lot. Ask about permits, site prep and utilities for your specific land early.
